Your finance options

Leasing or financing – the choice is yours.

Prefer to own your car, or drive it for an agreed term and return it? Explore the two options below.

Towards ownership

Balloon Finance

Pay an initial payment, then fixed monthly payments and an agreed final payment. Deferring part of the amount until the end can reduce your monthly payments compared with a fully amortising loan. The vehicle is yours once you've paid all amounts due.

  • Terms of 36, 48 or 60 months
  • Choose your initial payment
  • Agreed final payment
  • Own the vehicle once all amounts due are paid
Calculate your monthly payment →

A final payment is due at the end of the agreement. The vehicle is yours once all amounts due are paid.

Drive, then return

Operating Lease

Pay for the use of your vehicle over an agreed term, rather than buying it. Choose your term, annual mileage and initial payment before signing. At the end of the contract, return the vehicle in line with your agreement—without having to sell it yourself or worry about residual value.

  • Terms of 36, 48 or 60 months
  • Agreed annual mileage
  • Fixed monthly payments
  • No residual-value risk to you
Calculate your monthly rental →

Leasing does not grant ownership. Excess mileage and damage beyond normal wear may incur additional charges.

Frequently asked questions

Financing FAQs

How does Balloon Finance work?+

Choose your vehicle and an available payment plan, including your deposit, term and final payment. the financial partner then assesses your application. If approved, you sign the agreement and make fixed monthly payments, followed by the agreed final payment.

Why are the monthly payments lower?+

Part of the amount you borrow is left until the end of the agreement. This can reduce monthly payments compared with a loan that repays the full amount over the same term, but you'll still need to pay the final amount. Compare the total cost as well as the monthly payment.

What affects my monthly and final payments?+

Your vehicle price, deposit, agreement term, interest rate and agreed final payment affect your quote. Expected mileage may also be taken into account where the product uses an estimated end-of-term value. The terms in the financial partner's formal offer apply.

What happens at the end of Balloon Finance?+

Pay the final amount and any other outstanding charges to keep the car. Refinancing or switching to another vehicle may be available, subject to the products, terms and approval available at that time. A right to return the car is not automatic; check your agreement.

Can I repay early or change my agreement?+

You can ask the financial partner about early repayment or a change to your agreement. Available options, approval requirements and any charges depend on your contract. Terms cannot necessarily be changed after signing.

How does an Operating Lease work?+

You pay to use the car for an agreed period, rather than buying it. Your term, annual mileage and initial payment help determine the monthly rental. You normally return the car at the end, in accordance with the condition and mileage requirements in your agreement.

What if I drive more than the agreed mileage?+

Your mileage is checked when you return the car. Excess mileage is charged at the rate set out in your agreement. Any credit for driving fewer kilometres also depends on the contract.

Will I be charged for wear or damage?+

Your agreement explains the return standards. Fair wear and tear is treated differently from damage. Excessive wear, accident damage or missing items may result in additional charges following the vehicle inspection.

Do I need fully comprehensive insurance for a leased car?+

Motor third-party liability insurance is mandatory in Germany. Your lease may also require fully comprehensive cover (Vollkasko). Check the insurance requirements in your lease before taking delivery.

What if my finance application is declined?+

The financial partner makes the credit decision, and approval is not guaranteed. You can consider another payment method or contact Xiaomi Auto Financial Service for guidance on the next steps.
