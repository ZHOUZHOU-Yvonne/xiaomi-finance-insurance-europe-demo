Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about insurance

What's the difference between liability, partial comprehensive and fully comprehensive insurance?+

Motor third-party liability insurance (Kfz-Haftpflicht) covers injury or damage you cause to other people with your vehicle. Partial comprehensive cover (Teilkasko) typically protects against specified risks such as theft, glass damage and weather events. Fully comprehensive cover (Vollkasko) adds protection for accidental damage to your own vehicle and other insured risks. The exact cover and exclusions depend on the policy.

What EV-specific cover is available?+

Depending on the policy, cover may include the high-voltage battery, charging cables, wallbox equipment and recovery following an electrical incident. Check Allianz's policy wording for the insured events, limits and exclusions.

What is an eVB number?+

An eVB number is the reference for your electronic insurance confirmation (elektronische Versicherungsbestätigung). It provides proof of motor third-party liability insurance when you register a vehicle in Germany. Allianz will explain how to obtain it.

What affects my premium?+

Factors can include your vehicle, where you live, the drivers, annual mileage, no-claims class, chosen cover and excess. Allianz calculates your quote and confirms the price and terms.

What is an SF-Klasse?+

Your Schadenfreiheitsklasse, or no-claims class, reflects your claim-free driving history. It can affect the cost of motor third-party liability and fully comprehensive insurance in Germany.

How do I report an accident?+

Report the incident through Allianz's claims service. Have your policy number, vehicle registration number, incident details, photos and any police report ready. In an emergency, contact the emergency services first.

Am I covered when driving elsewhere in Europe?+

Cover and assistance depend on your policy and destination. Before travelling, check the territorial limits in your insurance documents and whether any additional documents or cover are needed.

Do I have to use an approved repairer?+

Some policies require repairs through an approved network. Check your policy for the repair arrangements and any requirements concerning original or equivalent-quality parts.

How do I change or cancel my policy?+

Contact Allianz to update driver, address, payment or vehicle details, or to cancel your policy. The policy documents explain the notice periods, cancellation rights and any other conditions.
